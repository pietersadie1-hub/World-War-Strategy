WORLD WAR STRATEGY

Command rivers, roads and armies. Conquer the frontier.

How to play

  • Goal: destroy the enemy Headquarters. Defend your own.
  • Select: click / drag a box. Order: right-click (or tap with selection) to move, attack, or capture.
  • Economy: build Mines on mineral fields ◆, Pumps by water, Power Plants and Dams for energy. Dams also bridge rivers.
  • Roads speed up your units — pave supply lines, bridge rivers.
  • Stances: Guard, Patrol, Assault, Hold — set attack readiness per squad.
  • Capture neutral town buildings with Engineers for bonus income.
  • Keys: WASD/edge pan, wheel zoom, A attack-move, P patrol, S stop, 1-9 groups (Ctrl+n assign), F5 quick-save, F9 quick-load, Esc cancel/menu.
